# Global Referendums 2026 - Comprehensive Research

## ALREADY HELD IN 2026

### Thailand - Constitutional Referendum (Feb 8, 2026) - PASSED
- Topic: Whether to begin process of rewriting the 2017 coup-era constitution
- Result: YES won (5.47M yes vs 2.96M no)
- Held alongside general election
- Source: Thai PBS World, East Asia Forum

### Bangladesh - Constitutional Referendum (Feb 12, 2026) - PASSED
- Topic: Institutional reforms proposed after 2024 political upheaval
- Result: Endorsed by voters alongside parliamentary elections
- BNP won two-thirds majority in concurrent election
- Source: IFES, BSG Oxford

### Italy - Constitutional Referendum (Mar 22-23, 2026) - HELD
- Topic: Nordio Reform - separating prosecutors and judges, restructuring judicial system
- Confirmatory referendum on PM Meloni's judicial reform
- Major political test for Meloni government
- Source: Jurist.org, La Civiltà Cattolica, Eurac Research

### Switzerland - Referendum (Mar 8, 2026) - HELD
- Topics: Multiple initiatives (details from earlier vote)
- Source: Swiss government (admin.ch)

### Switzerland - Referendum (Jun 14, 2026) - REJECTED
- Topic 1: "No to a Switzerland with 10 million!" (Sustainability Initiative) - population cap
- Topic 2: Related immigration initiative
- Result: Both REJECTED by voters
- Source: The Guardian, SRF, Swissinfo

### Ecuador - Constitutional Referendum (Nov 16, 2025) - REJECTED
- Topic: Multiple questions including hosting foreign military bases
- Result: All 4 questions REJECTED (60%+ No)
- Source: Al Jazeera, CEPR

## UPCOMING IN 2026

### Armenia - Constitutional Referendum (TBD, after Jun 7 elections)
- Topic: New constitution drafted by reform council; PM Pashinyan plans referendum post-elections
- Status: Date not confirmed; elections held Jun 7, referendum expected later in 2026
- Context: Moving away from post-Soviet constitutional framework
- Source: Azatutyun (RFE/RL), ConstitutionNet

### Slovakia - Referendum (Jul 4, 2026) - CONFIRMED
- Topic: Whether to cancel lifelong payments for PM Robert Fico and other former leaders
- Originally also included early elections question, but Constitutional Court struck that down
- Status: Confirmed, called by President Pellegrini
- Context: Only 2003 EU accession referendum has ever succeeded in Slovakia
- Source: Washington Post, Slovak Spectator

### Iceland - Constitutional Referendum (Aug 29, 2026) - TENTATIVE
- Topic: Adoption of new citizen-written constitution (dating back to 2012 process)
- Status: Date not confirmed
- Context: Long-running saga since 2008 financial crisis; parliament has repeatedly failed to act on 2012 referendum results
- Source: IFES, Verfassungsblog

### Switzerland - Referendum (Sep 27, 2026) - CONFIRMED
- Topic 1: Stricter neutrality initiative (would limit government ability to impose sanctions)
- Topic 2: Food security initiative
- Proposed by: SVP (Swiss People's Party) / isolationist group
- Government and parliament AGAINST the neutrality measure
- Context: Would prevent Switzerland from joining EU sanctions (e.g., against Russia)
- Source: Swissinfo/Bloomberg
