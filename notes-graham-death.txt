LINDSEY GRAHAM DEATH - VERIFIED RESEARCH (July 12, 2026)
==========================================================

FACTS (confirmed by NPR, NBC, CNN, NYT, WaPo, Politico, CBS):
- Lindsey Graham (R-SC) died Saturday evening July 11/12, 2026
- Age: 71
- Cause: "brief and sudden illness" per his office
- He was running for a 5th term (had already won the Republican primary one month ago)
- He was scheduled to appear on NBC's Meet the Press on Sunday
- He recently visited Ukraine touring a drone production facility
- Dispatch audio indicates EMS responded to cardiac arrest at his home

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT (per Townhall/Christian Heiens and WJCL):

1. GOVERNOR APPOINTMENT (INTERIM):
   - Gov. Henry McMaster (R) can appoint an interim senator immediately
   - SC does NOT require same-party appointment (but McMaster is R, so will appoint R)
   - Appointee serves until special election

2. REPLACEMENT NOMINEE (SC Republican Party):
   - Since Graham already won the primary, the SC GOP must choose a replacement nominee
   - Per SC election law timeline (from Christian Heiens @ChristianHeiens):
     * "On the second Tuesday after today, candidate filing opens for one week"
     * "The special primary is then held on the second Tuesday after filing closes"
     * So special primary would be around July 28, 2026
     * "If no candidate wins a majority, there is a runoff two weeks later"

3. GENERAL ELECTION:
   - The Republican replacement nominee will face Democrat Annie Andrews in the November 3 general
   - This was already rated Solid R before Graham's death

IMPLICATIONS FOR OUR MAP:
- SC Senate race: incumbent changes from "Lindsey Graham" to "TBD (R nominee)" 
- Status: needs to reflect the special primary process
- Democrat challenger: Annie Andrews (confirmed)
- Rating: likely remains Solid R (SC is deep red)
- Need to mark this as a special circumstance
- Current composition: R loses 1 seat temporarily until McMaster appoints interim
