Maryland Redistricting Special Session - Research Notes (Jul 8, 2026)

Key facts:
- Maryland General Assembly will convene special session August 3-5, 2026
- Purpose: Consider a constitutional amendment on congressional redistricting standards
- This is related to midcycle congressional redistricting (ahead of 2028 elections)
- Will NOT include new maps at this session - just the constitutional amendment
- The amendment would undo a 2022 court ruling (Judge Lynne Battaglia) that said 
  congressional districts must be compact, contiguous, and mindful of boundaries
- If amendment passes legislature, it goes to voters in November 2026 ballot
- If voters approve, new maps could be drawn for 2028 elections
- Current map favors Democrats in 7 of 8 districts
- Announced by Senate President Bill Ferguson and House Speaker Joseline Peña-Melnyk
- Democrats control the legislature (supermajority)
- Governor Wes Moore (D) in office
- Republicans oppose the special session

Status for redistricting map:
